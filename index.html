Coating — what the D and N suffixes mean

The coating is the last part of the rotor type: T 34 DD is a T-groove 34 mm rotor with DD coating.

D
Nickel-diamond coating for extended lifetime.
DD
A second diamond layer — the longest-lasting option.
N
Smooth nickel top layer for sensitive fibres. On its own, steel rotors only.
DN
D coating plus the smooth nickel top layer.
DDN
DD coating plus the smooth nickel top layer.

SolidRotor — types & service life

Single-piece rotor for Autocoro 8–11.

Expected service life > 35,000 h (DD coating). Warranty: 12 months / 8,000 h. Sandy or bleached cotton, man-made fibres with titanium oxide and viscose can reduce service life.

Rule of thumb — rotor groove type:

  • Knitting → G-type rotor
  • Weaving → T-type rotor
  • Coarser yarn counts → Tc / U-type rotor
