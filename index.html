Spinning Rotors

High-precision rotors engineered for ACO8, ACO9, ACO10 and ACO11 automatic rotor-spinning machines. High-end European nickel-diamond coating for >35,000-hour lifetimes, curved thin-wall design for lower power consumption and rapid start/stop, and consistent yarn quality across the full lifecycle.

Rotor Cups & Bearings
